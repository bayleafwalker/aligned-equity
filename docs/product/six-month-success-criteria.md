# Six-Month Success Criteria

This review asks whether Aligned Equity changed real decisions or non-decisions after six
months of use. It is intentionally not a traffic, model-count, or ranking-performance review.

## Decision Impact

The system is useful if at least one of these is true:

- It prevented a purchase, job application, supplier decision, or research commitment that
  would otherwise have proceeded.
- It caused a company to move from generic watchlist status into a concrete research state
  with cited evidence.
- It changed the timing or scope of follow-up research because evidence confidence or
  source freshness was visibly weak.
- It preserved a non-decision by showing that available evidence was too sparse or biased
  to support action.

## Evidence Quality

After six months, reviewed company files should show:

- source lineage for every material conclusion
- confidence and comparability caveats alongside scorecard outputs
- same-firm longitudinal comparisons where prior-period evidence exists
- explicit bias flags for people-platform signals
- event overrides separated from recurring score dimensions
- decision-output notes that state whether more research was likely to change action
- no unsupported causal claims in decision-facing scorecard or memo outputs

## Failure Conditions

The system has not met the bar if:

- decisions are still justified by ad hoc notes outside the evidence model
- confidence fields are ignored during lens output
- sparse legal or enforcement events dominate normal scoring
- people-platform signals are treated as primary truth
- broad rankings or predictive-looking scores influence decisions without validation tied to decision cost
- decision outputs imply causal effects without causal-design metadata
- HLA integration requires core-package runtime imports from homelab-analytics

## Review Artifact

The six-month review should produce one short decision-impact note with:

- decisions changed
- non-decisions preserved
- companies reviewed
- evidence gaps that mattered
- model or workflow changes required before the next phase
