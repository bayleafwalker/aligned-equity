# Product Vision

Aligned Equity helps maintain a longitudinal, evidence-backed view of how public-company governance, incentives, management systems, disclosure quality, and organizational behavior line up with long-term decision contexts.

The initial universe is Finland-heavy and source-first. The project should make same-firm change over time easier to see before it tries to produce broad cross-sectional rankings.

## Primary users

- Investment research: identify long-term candidates, dislocations, thesis changes, and avoid states.
- Workplace research: evaluate management quality, stability, integrity, and organizational risk signals.
- Product/customer research: evaluate conduct, reliability, support continuity, and service-risk signals.

## Phase 0 success criteria

- Evidence classes, lenses, and research states are closed vocabularies.
- Sources are treated by reliability and comparability, not by convenience.
- Homelab-analytics integration is explicit enough to build later without coupling this repo to HLA internals.
- After six months of use, the project should have materially changed the reasoning of at least three real decisions or non-decisions with traceable evidence.
