name: domain-impact-scan
description: Use when evaluating a new source family, evidence class, lens, or HLA publication surface.
---

## Goal

Identify the contracts and boundaries affected by a new domain or source family.

## Steps

1. Name the affected boundary: source inventory, evidence record, feature extraction,
   scorecard, lens output, research state, or HLA publication.
2. Identify governing docs, schemas, and tests.
3. Confirm whether the source is core evidence, auxiliary evidence, or event override.
4. List required docs, tests, and blockers.

## Do not

- Do not approve a change that makes people-platform signals core truth.
- Do not approve a change that makes sparse legal events a primary scored axis.
